## Frequently Asked Questions About Shogun's Reign Enemies

### What is the most effective way to defeat armored Shogun's Reign enemies?
Armored hostiles possess significant poise, rendering basic frontal attacks largely ineffective. To neutralize them, prioritize blunt weaponry like the Kanabo to shatter their guard, or utilize agile footwork to flank their rear where their armor rating and block coverage are lowest.

### Where do you encounter the most difficult Shogun's Reign enemies?
The most formidable **Shogun's Reign enemies** congregate within secluded mountain passes, contested border territories, and deep forest ruins. These regions feature high-tier rogue ronin, elite assassin factions, and environmental hazards designed to test experienced combatants.

### Can civilian roles like Farmers or Merchants survive encounters with hostile enemies?
Yes. Civilian classes can survive by relying on tactical evasion, group travel, and defensive utility tools like smoke bombs. Furthermore, forming protective contracts with combat-focused player factions ensures safe passage across dangerous delivery routes without needing to engage in direct combat.

### Does your species choice impact how Shogun's Reign enemies interact with you?
Yes. Lineage and species traits determine your baseline defensive stats, movement agility, and poise. Certain species receive natural resistances against specific damage types, while others excel at rapid disengagements, heavily shifting the advantage during hostile skirmishes.

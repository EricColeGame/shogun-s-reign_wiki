## Core Archetypes and Class Breakdown in Shogun's Reign

Troop management hinges on rock-paper-scissors combat mechanics adapted for Sengoku-era battlefields. Every regiment falls into a core combat archetype: frontline infantry, ranged support, shock cavalry, or specialized stealth and utility. 

Frontline infantry forms the backbone of any standing army. Ashigaru units are cheap to recruit and field in large numbers, absorbing charge damage and holding choke points. Samurai infantry, by contrast, feature superior armor ratings, higher morale thresholds, and lethal melee attack speeds designed to carve through lower-tier footmen.

Ranged units provide area denial and chip damage before lines clash. Bow units offer high firing arcs capable of clearing castle parapets and defensive palisades, while matchlock teppo gunners deliver armor-piercing volleys at close-to-medium range. Cavalry units supply mobility and flank-disrupting charge power, though community reports consistently warn against charging them directly into braced spear walls.

## Tactical Formations and Army Composition Strategies

Deploying powerful shogun s reign units without disciplined formation control leads to disjointed skirmishes where your troops get picked apart. Formations modify movement speed, directional armor, and line cohesion.

Player experience and tactical testing highlight three dominant tactical compositions:

### 1. The Crane Wing (Tsuru No Jin)
This formation spreads your frontline wide, pulling defensive infantry back in the center while pushing elite melee troops along the flanks. When the enemy charges the center, the wings fold inward, trapping hostile regiments in a devastating pincer attack. Use Katana Samurai on the wings and resilient Yari Ashigaru in the center pocket.

### 2. The Arrowhead (Gyorin)
An aggressive wedge designed to punch through fortified river crossings or narrow valley passes. Heavy cavalry and shock infantry spearhead the point, followed closely by matchlock support to execute immediate point-blank volleys once the front seam splits.

### 3. The Reclining Dragon (Goryu)
A defensive fortification formation optimal for holding hills and objective flags. Spearmen brace in an overlapping perimeter, with archers elevated behind them and shinobi hidden in adjacent tree lines to execute surprise rear ambushes when the enemy commits.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Shogun's Reign Units

### What are the best shogun s reign units for beginners?
The most reliable setup for new players combines **Yari Ashigaru** with **Bow Ashigaru**. Yari spearmen are cheap to recruit and easily counter aggressive cavalry charges when placed in a braced spear wall. Archers positioned safely behind the spear line provide consistent damage, allowing beginners to learn battle pacing and resource management without risking expensive samurai squads.

### How do I counter heavy cavalry charges effectively?
To neutralize cavalry charges, position your Yari Spearmen or Naginata Monks in a tightly packed formation and activate their braced defensive stance before impact. Never let cavalry catch your spearmen while marching; moving pikemen do not gain charge-reflection bonuses. Additionally, firing matchlock volleys into oncoming cavalry causes mount panic, breaking their charge momentum before they make contact.

### Can I recruit shinobi in standard army barracks?
No. Shinobi cannot be recruited through standard barracks or military camps. To unlock shinobi units, you must construct a specialized Shadow Dojo or upgrade your clan stronghold’s espionage tree. Shinobi cost higher gold reserves and have limited squad sizes, but their ability to remain invisible on the minimap makes them invaluable for eliminating high-value targets.

### How do upgrades affect unit upkeep costs?
Promoting your troops to Veteran and Master tiers increases their base combat statistics, but it also raises their per-minute upkeep costs by roughly 20% to 35% per tier. Be careful not to upgrade your entire standing army at once without securing additional resource nodes or tax revenue, or your clan economy may plunge into a deficit, causing unit desertion.

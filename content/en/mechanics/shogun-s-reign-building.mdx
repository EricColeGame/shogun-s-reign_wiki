## How to Claim a House and Start Building

Securing a plot in Edo Japan is the foundation of your architectural journey. In Shogun’s Reign, housing plots and vacant structures are scattered throughout the world, ranging from modest roadside shacks in rural farmlands to refined residences within town walls.

To claim a structure, navigate through the district where you wish to settle. Look for properties marked with an interaction prompt or a deed sign outside the entryway. Interacting with the claim node binds the structure to your session, allowing you to unlock doors, restrict visitor access, and open the placement interface. According to player experience, high-traffic plots near central marketplaces and dojos fill up rapidly upon server creation, making prompt exploration essential when joining a fresh server.

Once claimed, you can toggle building mode through the contextual user interface. This interface enables furniture placement, structural customization, and layout adjustments.

| Step | Action Required | Key Controls / Interaction | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Scout Locations** | Search districts for unoccupied dwellings | Walk through urban & rural zones | Pick an area matching your character's class |
| **2. Interact with Node** | Claim ownership of the plot | Press prompt key (`E` / Action button) | Check server population before investing time |
| **3. Access Build Menu** | Open your decoration and editing toolbox | Click the House/Build icon on UI | Clear default clutter before placing items |
| **4. Place Essentials** | Lay down floor mats, lights, and storage | Drag, rotate, and snap items to grid | Align mats along walls to prevent clipping |
| **5. Set Permissions** | Manage guest access and door locks | House Settings / Security tab | Restrict doors to friends during private roleplay |

## Interior Decoration: Mastering Traditional Edo Aesthetics

Creating an immersive Japanese interior requires more than randomly dropping furniture onto floorboards. Traditional Japanese architecture (*Washitsu*) relies on harmony, minimalism, natural materials, and deliberate spatial proportions.

The cornerstone of any authentic build is the proper arrangement of tatami mats. In traditional design, mats are laid in specific orientations to avoid intersecting corners that bring bad fortune. In the build menu, use the fine-rotation tool to ensure your floor coverings align neatly against shoji screen walls.

```
+------------------------------------------+
|            Tokonoma (Alcove)             |
|   [Hanging Scroll]     [Bonsai / Vase]   |
+--------------------+---------------------+
|                    |                     |
|    Tatami Mat 1    |    Tatami Mat 2     |
|    (Horizontal)    |     (Vertical)      |
|                    |                     |
+--------------------+---------------------+
|        Low Chabu-dai Table & Cushions    |
+------------------------------------------+
| [Shoji Screen Door]       [Shoji Screen] |
+------------------------------------------+
```

To elevate your interior, integrate key focal points throughout your rooms. Community reports highlight several essential decorative elements that make an estate feel authentic:

- **Tokonoma (Decorative Alcove):** A recessed space reserved for displaying art, hanging calligraphy scrolls, seasonal flower arrangements (*ikebana*), or ceramic incense burners.
- **Chabu-dai (Low Wooden Table):** The social center of the living area, surrounded by woven floor cushions (*zabuton*) where guests gather to converse and drink tea.
- **Shoji & Fusuma Partitions:** Sliding paper and wood screens used to divide large open spaces into functional private chambers, sleeping nooks, or armories.
- **Andon Lanterns:** Soft, paper-covered floor lanterns that provide warm ambient lighting during nighttime hours without harsh glare.
- **Kakejiku (Hanging Scrolls):** Wall decorations bearing poetic brushwork or naturalistic landscape paintings.

| Furniture Category | Common Items | Placement Suggestion | Aesthetic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flooring** | Tatami, dark cedar planks, stone pavers | Full coverage with clean margins | Establishes the room's formal status |
| **Seating & Tables** | Zabuton cushions, chabu-dai, low desks | Centered in the main gathering room | Promotes communal roleplay scenes |
| **Lighting** | Paper andon lanterns, hanging oil lamps | Corners and beside sliding entryways | Creates dramatic, moody evening shadows |
| **Storage & Utility** | Wooden tansu chests, weapon racks | Flush against solid back walls | Displays martial prowess or wealth |
| **Nature & Accents** | Potted bonsai, bamboo dividers, tea sets | Table tops, alcoves, and thresholds | Adds organic tranquility to interiors |

## System Mechanics, Saving, and Troubleshooting

Because Shogun's Reign is an interactive experience hosted on the [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com), housing mechanics are subject to server rules, instance persistence, and memory constraints. Knowing how the backend manages player data protects your creative efforts from accidental loss.

Community guides frequently emphasize that claimed houses remain tied to you only while you stay in the active server, unless the game provides a dedicated persistent save slot. Always check your game settings menu to verify whether automatic blueprint saving is enabled.

| Common Issue | Probable Cause | Recommended Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Furniture Snapping Errors** | Object collision overlapping with wall hitbox | Toggle off grid snap or decrease collision sensitivity |
| **Unsaved Layout on Rejoin** | Leaving server without manually saving blueprint | Use the "Save Layout" prompt before exiting the session |
| **Locked Out of Plot** | Session timer expired or server reset | Re-claim the plot or find an alternate vacant lot |
| **Item Limit Reached** | Plot has hit its maximum prop threshold | Remove invisible duplicates or small ground clutter |
| **Visitors Walking Through Walls** | High server ping causing client-side de-sync | Toggle door locks off and on to re-register collision |

To maintain optimal performance for yourself and nearby players, avoid stacking excessive light sources in a single room. Overlapping dynamic light radiuses can cause frame-rate drops on lower-end devices, ruining the visual presentation of an otherwise immaculate estate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock more furniture for Shogun's Reign building?
New furniture, decorative props, and architectural accents can be obtained by spending in-game currency earned through jobs (such as farming, guard duty, or crafting), completing seasonal tasks, or leveling up your character's standing within your chosen faction.

### Can other players steal items from my house?
No. Furniture and props placed inside your claimed dwelling are protected by the game's housing permissions. While players can enter if your doors are unlocked, they cannot pick up, move, or confiscate your placed decorations.

### What should I do if my Shogun's Reign building plot disappears?
If your plot resets or you lose ownership, it typically means the server restarted or your session timed out after a prolonged period of inactivity. Check your build menu to see if your layout was saved as a blueprint, which allows you to redeploy your saved design onto another vacant plot with a single click.

### Can I build a two-story residence in Shogun's Reign?
Yes, certain merchant properties and samurai estates in the town center feature pre-built second floors or loft spaces. You can customize the upper levels with sleeping mats, private study desks, and storage chests just as you would the ground floor.

## Choosing Your Path: Roles, Jobs, and Earning Money

Economic independence is a cornerstone of feudal progression. Without currency (commonly exchanged as coins or *sen*), purchasing custom outfits, tea ceremony supplies, weapons, and furniture remains out of reach.

For anyone researching **shogun s reign how to play** as a self-sufficient villager, selecting an accessible starting profession is crucial. Roles are not merely cosmetic titles; each profession features distinct mini-games, responsibilities, and progression paths that govern your daily routine.

### The Farmer: Consistent Baseline Income
Farming provides the most reliable starting loop in the game. You receive access to designated crop fields where you plow soil, plant seeds, irrigate plots, and harvest rice, vegetables, or tea leaves. Once collected, bundle your produce and deliver it to the provincial storehouse or sell it directly to shopkeepers. Player experience shows that farming for 20 minutes yields sufficient initial funds to purchase your first formal wardrobe upgrades.

### The Merchant: Market Mastery and Trade
Merchants drive the community-run economy. Rather than generating items from static nodes, merchants buy raw materials, convert them into trade commodities, or set up stalls to sell crafted equipment, dyes, and street food to other roleplayers. Success as a merchant requires active social engagement, clever pricing, and negotiation skills.

### The Attendant: Courtly Service and Palace Life
If you prefer political intrigue over dirt roads and market stalls, the Attendant profession places you within noble manors and administrative halls. Attendants prepare tea, maintain interior decor, announce visiting guests, and execute formal palace protocols. While physical tasks are fewer, leveling up as an Attendant requires strict adherence to court etiquette and active roleplay participation.

## Property and Housing: Claiming and Decorating Your Estate

Owning a homestead provides a private retreat to store belongings, host tea gatherings, and display decorative achievements. Understanding property acquisition is a major milestone when learning **shogun s reign how to play** over an extended campaign.

Houses are located throughout residential wards, mountain trails, and market alleyways. Vacant homes display an interactive wooden registry sign near their front entrance.

```
[Find Vacant House Marker] ➔ [Confirm Lease via Signboard] ➔ [Enter Build / Decor Mode] ➔ [Place Tatami & Lighting]
```

### How to Claim and Maintain a House
1. **Locate an Unclaimed Parcel:** Wander through the outer residential quarter and search for markers showing an "Available" or "For Lease" indicator.
2. **Pay the Initial Deposit:** Interact with the signboard. Depending on the size of the home (humble shack vs. sprawling courtyard estate), you must pay an upfront lease in provincial coins.
3. **Locking Permissions:** Access your door interface to set lock preferences, granting access to clan members or locking out unwanted intruders.
4. **Pay Recurring Upkeep:** Ensure your character bank holds sufficient currency to cover weekly property taxes, preventing eviction.

## Roleplay Etiquette and Progression Strategies for Beginners

Because this title relies heavily on community-driven immersion, mechanics alone will not guarantee a fulfilling experience. Following established roleplay conventions will help you integrate smoothly into established server factions.

### 1. Separate In-Character (IC) from Out-of-Character (OOC)
Always distinguish between your character's dialogue and your personal commentary. Use parentheses `((like this))` or dedicated OOC chat channels when discussing real-world subjects or asking mechanical questions. ### 2. Respect Hierarchical Ranks
Edo-era Japan was governed by strict social stratifications. If your character is an apprentice farmer or traveling peddler, bow respectfully to ranking samurai, municipal magistrates, and noble attendants. Ignoring social customs may lead to in-game arrest, expulsion from town districts, or dueling challenges.

### 3. Progressive Wealth Building
Avoid attempting to purchase luxury weapons immediately. Follow a steady progression track:


Community reports suggest joining a specialized Discord or community group associated with the server. Connecting with active factions grants access to organized clan conflicts, ceremonial banquets, and trade caravans that solo players frequently miss out on.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Shogun's Reign

### What should I do first when learning shogun s reign how to play?
When discovering **shogun s reign how to play**, your immediate priority should be completing your visual customization, choosing an accessible role like Farmer or Attendant, and learning the district map. Focusing on a basic job allows you to accumulate starting coins for better clothing and household leases before diving into complex political roleplay.

### How do I earn money quickly as a solo player?
Farming is widely considered the most reliable starting method for solo players. Harvesting rice paddies and turning in crates at the municipal storehouse delivers guaranteed currency payouts without requiring trading partners or specialized gear.

### Can other players enter my house without permission?
If your house door is locked through the residential management menu, unauthorized players cannot open the door or interact with your stored containers. Be sure to check your door lock status every time you log back into a server.

### What happens if I change my character's role?
You can re-register your occupation at the central registry board. While some roles require returning specialized equipment or relinquishing municipal titles, your personal wallet, clothing inventory, and owned properties carry over between professions.

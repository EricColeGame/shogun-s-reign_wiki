## Frequently Asked Questions About the Shogun's Reign Map

### How do I navigate the Shogun's Reign map as a new player?
When you first load in, locate the massive central castle on the horizon to orient yourself toward the north. Head directly south down the paved main street to reach the Merchant Quarter, where you can pick up starter tools and speak to employers for introductory jobs. Following paved roads is the easiest way to discover all primary features on the **Shogun's Reign map** without getting turned around.

### Where are the main role-specific work stations located?
Work stations are concentrated within their thematic districts. Forging and carpentry benches are clustered on the western side of the Merchant Quarter. Attendant cleaning stations and laundry basins reside inside the Bathhouse facility, while administrative desks for clerical or guard duties are located on the ground floor of the Castle Barracks.

### Is the entire map open to all players from the start?
Most public sectors, including the commercial streets, performance pavilion, residential lanes, and outer gates, are entirely free to explore from your first login. However, specific high-security wings—such as the Shogun's personal throne room, council war rooms, and military armories—require specific clan ranks, faction permissions, or official audience invitations to enter.

### How do I purchase a plot in the residential district?
To claim a housing plot, navigate to the eastern residential wards and locate an unoccupied land marker displaying a wooden claim placard. Interact with the post to inspect the plot's tier, dimensions, and purchase cost. Ensure you have accumulated sufficient funds through merchant sales or employment earnings before finalizing your lease.

### The Agricultural Path: Farmer

The Farmer role is widely regarded as the most accessible starting career. By tending rice paddies, harvesting seasonal crops, and transporting bushels to market, you generate a dependable income while learning the map layout.

*   **Apprentice Farmer:** Unlocks standard hoes, basic crop seeds, and access to communal plots.
*   **Field Hand:** Increases harvest yield speed and reduces stamina drain per action.
*   **Master Cultivator:** Grants access to premium cash crops and private irrigation plots.

### The Commercial Path: Merchant

Merchants drive the server's economy by moving goods between producers and consumers. As you conduct business, you unlock expanded market stall licenses, wholesale catalogs, and currency counting aids like the abacus.

### The Service Path: Attendant

Attendants operate within estates, temples, and tea houses, serving influential figures and maintaining public venues. Working as an Attendant levels up your social etiquette stat, granting tips, exclusive domestic uniforms, and access to private clan compounds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the fastest shogun s reign unlocks for new players?
The quickest progression route for newcomers is joining the Farmer or Attendant career paths. Both roles provide immediate access to basic work equipment without upfront fees, allowing you to earn steady currency right away. Securing these initial shogun s reign unlocks gives you the financial foundation needed for species rerolls, housing deeds, and refined fashion items.

### Do species unlocks carry over between character resets?
According to player reports, species traits unlocked via special tokens or account-bound achievements remain available on your profile menu. However, if you manually reset your character's life within standard roleplay, you may need to re-earn role-specific ranks and property deeds depending on the server's reset rules.

### Can I own multiple properties at the same time?
In most standard gameplay loops, players are limited to one primary residential deed per character to prevent territory hoarding. However, advanced players can often lease additional commercial stalls or guild spaces if they achieve high standing in the Merchant guild.

### How do job promotions impact my daily gameplay?
Advancing in your career increases your base wage, unlocks superior tools that complete tasks faster, and grants access to exclusive work-related uniforms. Higher ranks also grant permission to enter restricted areas, such as clan compounds and administrative council halls.

Focus your early hours on low-barrier labor within the town. Gather supplies, assist established shopkeepers, and purchase basic seasonal clothing to replace default civilian rags. Avoid spending currency on expensive weapons during this stage.

### Phase 2: Secure Professional Licensing
Once you have accumulated a modest reserve of funds, invest in role-specific training or certifications. Becoming an apprentice entertainer or a junior town watchman opens consistent avenues for roleplay engagement and community tips.

### Phase 3: Equip Specialized Tools
With a steady role secured, spend your capital on tool-based **Shogun's Reign upgrades**. Acquire an authentic instrument if pursuing the arts, or secure a reliable katana and proper binding ropes if pursuing martial roles. This is also the ideal time to unlock additional outfit slots to store alternate character appearances.

### Phase 4: Seek Clan Affiliation and Estate Influence
The final stage of progression involves joining or establishing a recognized family household. High-tier upgrades at this level include owning district property, commanding retainers, and participating in high-stakes diplomatic councils.

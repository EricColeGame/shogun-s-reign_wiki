### Mastering the Drag and Carry Mechanics

The carry system features dynamic physics that respond directly to terrain elevation and obstacle placement. When dragging a target, avoid sprinting up steep stairs or through narrow sliding shōji doors, as sudden physics collisions can break the grip or cause erratic character repositioning. Always face forward and maintain a moderate pace when escorting someone to safety or holding cells.

## Pro Tips for Fluid Roleplay and Combat Optimization

Fine-tuning your Shogun's Reign controls can prevent costly input delays during high-stakes roleplay trials or fast-paced skirmishes. Dedicated community members utilize several practical configurations to streamline their experience:

*   **Separate Shift Lock from Sprint:** If your client binds Shift Lock and Sprint to the same key, reassign Shift Lock in your base client settings to avoid accidental camera snapping during chases.
*   **Use Chat Commands for Quick Emotes:** Instead of opening the emote wheel during formal court proceedings, type direct slash commands like `/e bow`, `/e sit`, or `/e kneel` into the chat bar for seamless etiquette transitions.
*   **Mind Your Draw Cooldown:** Drawing a weapon has a recovery animation. Never initiate the draw key (`R`) while an opponent is actively mid-swing, as you cannot parry until the blade is fully gripped.
*   **Stamina Conservation:** Sprinting constantly empties your gauge, leaving your character breathless and temporarily vulnerable to physical knockdowns. Toggle walking (`Ctrl`) inside indoor spaces to maintain maximum stamina reserves.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Shogun's Reign Controls

### How do I pick up or drag another player in Shogun's Reign?
To drag or carry another character, approach a downed or ragdolled player and press `V` to initiate a ground drag, or press `B` (or select the carry prompt via the `E` interaction menu) to hoist them onto your shoulder. To release the player, press the key a second time or jump.

### Can I rebind Shogun's Reign controls on PC?
While many core contextual hotkeys (like `E` for interaction and `R` for weapon readying) are hardcoded into the game's custom user interface, universal movement keys and camera settings can be adjusted through the standard client settings menu accessed by pressing `Escape`.

### What are the Shogun's Reign controls for drawing and sheathing weapons?
To unsheathe your katana or secondary arm, press `R` or press the corresponding number key on your hotbar (usually slot `1`). Pressing `R` again or pressing your unequip hotkey (`Z` or backtick) safely sheathes the blade back into its scabbard.

### How do I open the character customization and code loadout menu?
Open the clothing and customization interface by pressing the mirror or coat hanger icon located on the left or top edge of your screen, or press `M` in designated town dressing rooms. From there, you can paste custom save codes into the text box to load community-designed kimono styles and accessories.
